Rebirth During the last year, among all my investigations in fashion, one of Comme des Garçons' shows really stayed in my mind, Body Meets Dress, Dress Meets Body, I watched a couple of reviews about it, because Mrs. Rei Kawakubo always leaves us with so many question marks! A few months later, I came across Aloïse Design and her approach to clothing as an extension of the body. It instantly reminded me of that same show. That connection sparked something in me, so I reached out to her with an idea that I felt really aligned with her brand identity. The concept I shared was inspired by the myth of the goddess INANNA, her journey into the underworld and her transformation. I used it as a metaphor for death, rebirth, and renewal, which became the foundation of the project. After visiting a cemetery in Italy, I was struck by how different it was from the ones in my home country. That contrast made me think, why not shoot there? A cemetery perfectly captures the symbolism of death and transformation, which felt just right for the story we wanted to tell. And that's how this story began!
