About Me

I transitioned from Architecture to Fashion, immersing myself in the world of styling in search of my true calling. Today, I channel this journey into creating visual stories that reflect transformation, identity, and emotion.

Portfolio

Styling

Rebirth

×

Rebirth is a visual narrative inspired by transformation. Influenced by Comme des Garçons' "Body Meets Dress, Dress Meets Body" and Aloïse Design’s conceptual pieces, I proposed a collaborative shoot built around the myth of the goddess Inanna and her descent into the underworld—a metaphor for death, rebirth, and renewal. Shot in an Italian cemetery, the setting underscores the story’s symbolism, contrasting with the imagery from my own cultural background. It became a journey of shedding, surrendering, and becoming anew.

Cocoon

×

Cocoon portrays the quiet, internal struggle of self-growth. It’s a personal exploration of stagnation, stillness, and the courage to move forward when hope is scarce. The project reflects those moments of solitude and surrender, but also the slow, delicate act of healing—of wrapping yourself in stillness before emerging renewed. It’s an ode to the unseen effort behind becoming.

Architecture

×

Architecture formed the foundation of my creative practice. It taught me how to think spatially, construct narratives, and approach every project with structure and intention. My architectural background informs my work as a stylist—blending precision, storytelling, and emotional resonance.

Fashion Showroom

×

Showroom Experience gave me a hands-on understanding of fashion presentation and client relations. Supporting the setup and styling of showroom looks, I sharpened my eye for detail, organization, and how styling intersects with sales strategy and brand identity. It was an immersive behind-the-scenes experience that added depth to my styling approach.
